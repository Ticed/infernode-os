hear - Listen and transcribe speech (speech-to-text)

Usage:
  hear                       Listen and transcribe (default 5s)
  hear <duration_ms>         Listen for specified duration

Arguments:
  duration_ms - Recording duration in milliseconds (default: 5000)

Examples:
  hear                       Listen for 5 seconds
  hear 10000                 Listen for 10 seconds

Requires /n/speech (run speech9p first).
Each start charges a cumulative microphone budget (cat /n/speech/budget).
Once remaining is 0, further hear calls are refused until the operator
replenishes /n/speech/policy/ctl. API-backed hear is off unless the
operator has allowed it.
